## Show notes

Co-hosts Lisa Cao and Scott Haines present their Thanksgiving-week episode that's lighter on releases and heavier on proposals, with Spark, Iceberg and Parquet doing most of the talking. The thread running through it is hardening: governance moving into the engine, specs pinning down how views and expressions behave across engines, and formats paying off old compatibility debts.

- GTA 6's 210 GB pre-download leads to a conversation about digital distribution, bandwidth costs, and why compression and multimodal data storage are turning into data engineering problems.
- Quick hits: delta-rs 1.7.0, DuckDB 2.0.1, Lakekeeper 0.15.1 with console 0.31.0, Omnigent 0.23, Kimi K2.5, and a Gluten release candidate. Lisa suggests the holiday quiet on the dev lists is partly people holding proposals until someone's around to respond.
- The vote to extend Spark's real-time mode to distributed batch SQL passed. Lisa imagines Spark picking its own execution mode (Project Feather, real-time, micro-batch or batch), and the hosts dig into misconfigured and agent-generated benchmarks and who should fund fair ones.
- A revised SPIP for pluggable row filter and column mask enforcement in Catalyst would give catalogs one supported API instead of session extensions. A related SPIP explores parallel reads through Spark Connect, making Connect the boundary between untrusted compute and enforcement.
- From the Spark dev list: two prototypes for routing Iceberg materialized views in Spark (catalog-based vs. an analyzer rule), and a new, not-yet-shepherded SPIP on row-order-independent non-deterministic expressions, targeting Spark 5.
- delta-rs 1.7.0 adds streamed merges with target-file pruning, reporting 1.9x faster merges and 58% lower peak memory on a 10,000-file table.
- Iceberg's materialized view and UDF work moves into the spec, alongside a v4 discussion on cross-engine determinism for functions in stored expressions. Scott argues Iceberg will stay a table format, as v4 moves to drop ORC, keep Avro, and debate room for Lance or Vortex.
- Parquet threads: fixing parquet-java's v2 page encryption in 1.19 even if older readers break, a first security policy that treats Parquet files as untrusted data, and removing the 8 KB page size recommendation.
- The hosts close with what they're thankful for and preview the DataFusion and Arrow release votes landing next week.
