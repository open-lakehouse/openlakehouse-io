## Why MLflow for agents?

Agentic systems are messy: tool calls, retries, multi-step reasoning. MLflow
gives you **traces**, **evaluation harnesses**, and **versioned prompts** in
one open-source package.

Start with `mlflow.langchain.autolog()` and you'll instantly see every step
of your agent's reasoning in the UI.
