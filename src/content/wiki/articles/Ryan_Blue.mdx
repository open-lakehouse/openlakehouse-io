**Ryan Blue** is a software engineer known as one of the original creators of [Apache Iceberg](/wiki/Apache_Iceberg), an [open table format](/wiki/Open_table_format) for large analytic datasets. He developed Iceberg at [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) with Daniel Weeks, co-founded the data management company [Tabular](/wiki/Tabular) around the format, and joined [Databricks](/wiki/Databricks) when it acquired Tabular in 2024.[^prnewswire][^dbxblog]

## Career and work

Blue worked at Cloudera before joining Netflix, where he was part of the big data platform team.[^dais] At Netflix, Blue and Daniel Weeks developed Apache Iceberg and later donated the project to the [Apache Software Foundation](/wiki/Apache_Software_Foundation).[^dbxblog]

In 2021, Blue co-founded Tabular with Daniel Weeks and Jason Reid.[^techcrunch] Tabular built a data management platform around Iceberg, and Blue served as its chief executive officer.[^prnewswire]

On 4 June 2024, Databricks announced that it had agreed to acquire Tabular.[^prnewswire] Databricks stated that the acquisition completed on 7 June 2024.[^dbxblog] After the acquisition, Blue is listed as a Member of Technical Staff at Databricks.[^dais]

## Contributions to the open lakehouse

Apache Iceberg is the contribution for which Blue is most widely recognized. Iceberg was created at Netflix to address correctness, performance and scalability problems with the earlier [Apache Hive](/wiki/Apache_Hive) table layout, and was donated to the Apache Software Foundation as an open-source project.[^prnewswire][^dbxblog] Iceberg lets multiple query engines read and write the same tables on object storage with transactional guarantees, a pattern central to the [open lakehouse](/wiki/Open_lakehouse).

Blue chairs the Apache Iceberg Project Management Committee (PMC).[^prnewswire] The Apache Software Foundation lists him as Vice President, Apache Iceberg, the officer role that corresponds to the PMC chair of a top-level project.[^asf]

The 2024 Databricks acquisition was described as bringing together the original creators of Apache Iceberg and the creators of [Delta Lake](/wiki/Delta_Lake), with a stated goal of improving interoperability between the two open table formats.[^prnewswire][^dbxblog]

## Selected works

- Apache Iceberg, the open table format Blue co-created at Netflix and contributed to the Apache Software Foundation.[^prnewswire][^dbxblog]
- Conference presentations on Apache Iceberg and open table formats, including sessions at the Databricks Data + AI Summit.[^dais]
